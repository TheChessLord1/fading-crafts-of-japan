Who am I?

Hi, I'm Aashray! I'm a Grade 9 student. In my free time, I love playing chess and football, and I'm a big fan of gaming. When I was younger, I was really into Pokémon, which was one of the first things that got me interested in Japanese culture. I enjoy learning about how things work and creating projects like this website.

Why I chose this issue

I've always been interested in Japan. It's a country where modern technology and centuries-old traditions exist side by side, which I find really cool. My favourite traditional craft is origami, because it shows how something as simple as a single sheet of paper can be turned into something amazing with skill and patience. When I started researching, I was surprised to learn that many of Japan's traditional crafts are slowly disappearing because fewer young people are learning them. I chose this issue because I think it's important that these skills aren't lost forever, and I wanted to help more people find out about them.

Hopes and goals

My main hope is that this website helps people learn about traditional Japanese crafts and understand why they are in danger of disappearing. I want visitors to leave knowing more about crafts they may never have heard of, and to feel inspired to support them, whether by buying handmade items, trying a craft workshop, or simply sharing what they learned. My goal is to show that crafts can be saved if people care about them, just like some crafts that were lost and later brought back. Through this project, I have also learned a lot about research, design and building websites, and I hope to keep improving these skills in the future.
